# Close

Open the roadmap item and its progress entries together.

1. Record what was Done in `progress.md`.
2. Add the exact evidence under Verified. A completed action without evidence stays Open or In progress.
3. Update the roadmap status to `Verified` only when the evidence listed there was checked.
4. Write any remaining work as Open and name the Next action.

Use these words plainly: Done, Verified, Open, Next. They make handoffs readable and expose wishful completion.
