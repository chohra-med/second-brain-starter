Where it lives

S1Where should your Second Brain live?

It is one folder of plain text files: your projects, decisions and notes. Keep it apart from the starter copy you downloaded.

Why we recommend A new folder named Second Brain, inside your Documents folder: A new, empty folder is the simplest start: nothing of yours can be overwritten.

What happens: The assistant repeats the exact folder in the chat and asks you to confirm it before anything is written.

Example

After setup, the folder holds Home.md, the page you open first, and six numbered folders such as 01-Projects and 05-Daily.

S2Which apps will open this workspace?

Both apps find the skills and the rules on their own. Some other tools need one setting.

Why we recommend the ones marked Recommended: Both work with no extra setting, so picking both costs nothing.

What happens: For another tool, the assistant shows you the one setting it needs and adds it only after your yes.

Example

Gemini CLI needs one line that tells it to read the rules file. Cursor and Copilot CLI need nothing.

S3Add the optional skill for websites without an API?

This skill, api-anything, lets the assistant use a website that has no export button and no API.

Why we recommend Not now: When used, it downloads an outside command and can read cookies from your everyday browser. Add it later if you need it.

What happens: If you say yes, the assistant copies the skill into both skill folders after setup.

Example

Reading your own saved articles from a website that has no export button.
